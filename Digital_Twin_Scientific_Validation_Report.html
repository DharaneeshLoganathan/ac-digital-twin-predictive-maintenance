🔍 3. Deep-Dive Physical Derivations & Paper References
1. Compressor Dome Thermal Limit (DS18B20 on GPIO 5) Normal: 45-65°C | Warning: ≥68°C | Critical: ≥80°C
• Physical Mechanism: Hermetic rotary/scroll compressors rely on cool suction vapor (4°C-10°C) to cool the motor stator. When condenser fouling elevates condensing pressure or gas leaks reduce mass flow, top shell temperature climbs.
• OEM Specification: Copeland Application Engineering Bulletin AE4-1365, Section 4.2 specifies that continuous dome temperatures must not exceed 85°C to protect polyolester (POE) oil viscosity. The internal thermal cutoff (Klixon OLP) trips at 105°C-115°C. Our predictive alert at 80°C-85°C prevents equipment damage before hard-tripping.
• Direct Literature Link: Emerson Climate Bulletin AE4-1365 (PDF)
2. Zone Temperature Split / Cooling Delta (ΔT) Normal: 6-12°C | Critical Leak: ≤ 2.5°C
• Physical Mechanism: In a healthy vapor-compression cycle, the enthalpy differential across the evaporator produces a 7°C-11°C air temperature drop between outdoor ambient and room supply.
• NIST & AHRI Reference: NISTIR 7044 (Sensitivity Analysis of FDD Methods, Section 3.2, pp. 18-22) and AHRI Standard 210/240 establish that a refrigerant charge loss of ≥30% reduces sensible capacity by >65%, causing ΔT to collapse below 2.5°C.
• Direct Literature Link: NIST Technical Report NISTIR 7044 (PDF)
3. Decoupled Thermodynamic Heat Lift (T_comp - T_ambient) Normal: 10-18°C | Warning: ≥ 20°C | Critical: ≥ 40°C
• Physical Mechanism: Heat Lift measures condenser heat rejection resistance. Fouled condenser fins reduce ambient heat exchange, directly elevating compressor discharge head temperature over ambient air.
• Academic Reference: Li & Braun, International Journal of Refrigeration (Elsevier, Vol. 30, No. 6, pp. 981-995) proves that (T_comp - T_outdoor) isolates condenser heat exchange faults from indoor airflow restrictions.
• DOI Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrefrig.2007.01.006
4. Motor Overcurrent & Overload Protection (16A Smart Plug) Nominal RLA: 3.5-5.2A | Warning: ≥ 6.8A | Critical: ≥ 7.0-7.8A
• Physical Mechanism: In 1.5-Ton (18,000 BTU/h) systems @ 230V, rated current is 4.8A-5.2A. High head pressure or grid voltage sag forces the motor to operate past Maximum Continuous Current (MCC).
• AHRI & LG Standards: AHRI Standard 540, Section 5.3 and LG Multi-V Service Manual, Section 7 confirm current >7.0A (>140% RLA) induces rotor thermal overload.
• Official Standard Link: AHRI Standard 540 Catalogue
5. Mechanical Vibration Anomaly (SW-420 on GPIO 27) Nominal: NORMAL (0) | Critical: VIBRATION DETECTED (1)
• Physical Mechanism: Compressors are dynamically balanced on rubber anti-vibration isolation grommets. Abnormal mechanical oscillation indicates rubber pad aging, loose bolts, outdoor fan unbalance, or bearing spalling.
• ISO Standard: ISO 10816-3 (Vibration Severity Guidelines for Class I/II Industrial Machines, Table C.1) mandates that vibration velocity >2.8 mm/s requires immediate corrective maintenance.
• Standard Catalogue Link: ISO 10816-3 Official Standard
